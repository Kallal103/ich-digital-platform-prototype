Stability Data Assessment Report

ICH Q12 Post-Approval Changes — Long-Term Stability Study

Sponsor: Pfizer Inc. · Protocol: PF-0724891 · Date: October 2026

3. Results — 36-Month Stability Data

The 36-month stability data for the proposed commercial formulation demonstrate consistent quality attributes within the established specification limits across all tested conditions (25°C/60% RH, 30°C/65% RH, and 40°C/75% RH accelerated).

No significant trends were observed for assay, degradation products, dissolution, or water content. All individual and mean values met the acceptance criteria specified in Section 2.3.
