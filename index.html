Terms of Use & Code of Conduct

Please review and accept before accessing the platform

1. Purpose

The ICH Digital Platform is a temporary, secure collaboration space for regulatory harmonisation activities. All data shared on this platform is subject to the governance framework established by the ICH Management Committee.

2. Confidentiality

All Commercially Confidential Information (CCI) uploaded by sponsors remains under the sponsor's control. You must not attempt to share, distribute, or export CCI data beyond the authorised parties designated by the data owner.

3. Data Protection

The platform complies with GDPR, PIPEDA, LGPD, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Personal data is processed solely for platform administration. Data controllers retain responsibility for their respective content.

4. Acceptable Use

Users must use the platform solely for authorised ICH harmonisation activities. Any misuse, unauthorised access attempts, or data exfiltration will be reported to the ICH Management Committee and may result in immediate suspension.

5. Encryption & Key Custody

CCI data is encrypted using envelope encryption with user-managed keys. The platform operator and ICH administrators have zero access to decrypted CCI content. Key holders are responsible for the custody and security of their encryption keys.

6. Temporary Nature

The platform is a transient collaboration space, not a permanent repository. All data is subject to defined retention periods and will be deleted through crypto-shredding at the end of each collaboration lifecycle.
